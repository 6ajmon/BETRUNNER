## About

**BETRUNNER** is a first-person parkour platformer built for the **GMTK 2026 Game Jam** (theme: *Countdown*). Developed in **Godot 4.7 with C#**, it turns the classic timer into a strategy layer: every level runs on **time you bet yourself**.

Before each level, a cinematic orbit camera shows you the course. You decide how many seconds out of your shared time pool you're willing to wager on beating it. Finish inside the bet and you keep your time. Overshoot — and the excess is **doubled** and drained from your pool, hurting both this run and the next level's budget. Run out of time mid-level and it's game over.

Can you read your own skill well enough to keep betting, keep moving, and survive the whole facility?

## How to play

- **Bet your time** — each level adds base time to your pool (minus any carried-over penalty), then you slide the bet bar before starting.
- **Run the course** — fast first-person platforming with sprint, jump (coyote time + jump buffering), wall jumps and climb boosts.
- **Survive the obstacles** — dodge lasers, cross moving platforms, trigger pressure plates and hit checkpoints along the way.
- **The countdown runs your clock** — the timer starts ticking the moment you step into the start trigger; touch the finish to stop it.
- **Manage your pool** — bonuses when you come in under your bet, doubled penalties when you overshoot. The pool is your real health bar.
- **Learn the score** — a full breakdown after every level, plus a time-graph history of your whole run on the end screen.

## Difficulty

| Difficulty | Time pool per level (approx.) | Best for |
|-----------|-------------------------------|----------|
| **Noob**  | Generous (60s → 40s → …)      | Learning the ropes and the betting meta |
| **Pro**   | Tighter (30s → 20s → …)       | The intended challenge |
| **Dev**   | Brutal (13s → 10s → …)        | A true countdown gauntlet |

The difficulty can be changed from the main menu before a run (locked during a run).
